How ViralTime AI works

A transparent look at how we estimate your best time to post — and what the results do and don't mean.

01

Choose your platform

Select TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, or YouTube Shorts. Each platform has its own general activity pattern.

02

Select country & content type

Pick your country from 195+ options and your content category. The tool uses your country's timezone to show windows in local time.

03

Get your posting window

See your estimated best time, an AI engagement score, alternative windows, and a 7-day outlook — all in your local time.

What the engine considers

The recommendation engine combines several inputs to produce an estimated posting window:

  • Platform — each platform has different general peak-activity hours.
  • Country & timezone — recommendations are shown in the selected country's local time.
  • Day of week — weekdays and weekends follow different activity curves.
  • Content category — categories like Gaming or Fitness shift the peak window earlier or later.
  • General audience activity patterns — broad, public patterns of when audiences tend to be online.

The "Post Now" system

When you check "Today", the tool compares your current local time with the recommended window. If you're inside the window, it shows a green "POST NOW" status. If not, it shows how long until the next best window opens. This is a convenience indicator — posting outside the window can still perform well.

What the AI engagement score means

The score (out of 100) is a relative estimate of audience activity for that window, not a prediction of views or engagement. A higher score means the window is estimated to have more audience activity, based on the inputs above. It is not a guarantee of any outcome.

What ViralTime AI does not do

  • It does not access your social media accounts, analytics, or passwords.
  • It does not collect or transmit personal data.
  • It does not guarantee viral views, followers, or engagement.
  • It does not predict how the platform's algorithm will treat your specific content.

Why results are estimates

Actual performance depends on content quality, audience, platform algorithm, competition, trends, and many other factors. Posting time is one lever among many. Use these estimated windows to give good content its best chance — and always prioritise content quality first.
