Privacy Policy

Your data never leaves your device.

Last updated: January 2026

Overview

ViralTime AI is a client-side web application. All core functionality runs entirely in your browser. There is no backend server, no database, and no third-party analytics. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal information.

What we collect

We do not collect personal data. The tool does not require an account, sign-up, or login. We do not use cookies to track you.

The optional username field

The checker includes an optional username field. Anything you type there stays on your device and is never sent to any server. It is not stored, not transmitted, and not used for any purpose beyond display. You can leave it blank.

No social media account access

ViralTime AI does not connect to your social media accounts. It does not ask for, store, or transmit passwords, tokens, or any account credentials. It does not access private analytics from TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, or any other platform.

Local storage

We use your browser's localStorage to remember your light/dark theme preference. This data is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or any third party. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

How recommendations work

All recommendations are calculated in your browser using your selected platform, country, timezone, day, and content category combined with general audience activity patterns. No data about your selections is transmitted anywhere.

Third-party services

ViralTime AI does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Share buttons link directly to the respective platforms (WhatsApp, Facebook, X) and only send the text you choose to share.

Children's privacy

ViralTime AI is intended for general use and does not knowingly collect any data from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the tool handles data, we will update this page. Because the tool is fully client-side, any change would only take effect in new versions of the website.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us.
